I am an undergraduate student at Dartmouth College, double majoring in Economics and Mathematics. At Dartmouth, I was a research assistant for Professors Robert Staiger and Andrew Levin. My undergraduate thesis, advised by Professor Treb Allen, analyzed the effect of demography and human capital on structural change and was awarded High Honors. I have also completed internships in the Macro Research division at Goldman Sachs and at the World Trade Organization.

I will start a Ph.D. in Business Economics at Harvard University (joint with Harvard Business School) in the fall of 2026. My research interests lie broadly in macroeconomics, international economics, and finance.
