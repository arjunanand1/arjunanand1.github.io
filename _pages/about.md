I am a Business Economics PhD student at Harvard University (joint program with Harvard Business School). My research interests lie broadly in macroeconomics, international economics, and finance. I graduated from Dartmouth College in 2026 with a B.A. in Economics and Mathematics.

At Dartmouth, I was a research assistant for Professors Robert Staiger and Andrew Levin. My undergraduate thesis, advised by Professor Treb Allen, analyzed the effect of demography and human capital on structural change. I have also completed internships in the Macro Research division at Goldman Sachs and at the World Trade Organization.
